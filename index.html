Colton Kirby

I am an undergraduate in my fourth year of studying philosophy and theology and pursuing a B.A. at George Fox University, where I am also part of the university's Honors College.

I am interested in metaphysics, epistemology, and Aristotelianism. More specifically, I am interested in whether there are "real categories" in the world and, if there are, how we could know them. I also maintain an enduring interest in the philosophy of religion and political philosophy.

When I am not doing philosophy, I will often be spending time with my wife (married 3yr) and our daughter (17 mo). We enjoy visiting local coffee shops, going on walks, watching movies, and staying involved at our local parish.
